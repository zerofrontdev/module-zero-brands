# Changelog: zerofront/module-zero-brands

## 1.3.6

- Brand logos are JPEG, PNG or WebP only; SVG is refused (it can carry script), and the file's content is checked, not only its extension. Logos uploaded before keep showing.
- Brand pages and product brands load without one query per product, and an unknown brand address no longer builds the whole brand list.

Earlier releases (1.3.5 and before): see the Git tags.
